# Every change goes to the activity log, recap, 2026-11-10

Written before the branch `audit-bff` merges into `main`; its base is `f1e5363f`, its plan `docs/plans/2026-11-10-audit-bff.md`.

## What happened

Before this piece, no change anyone made in the app was recorded. Now every change a person or an operator makes to a client's data is sent to the audit service with who made it, when, which record, and the record before and after. A route list test fails on any new route that changes data and neither writes an entry nor names why it does not.

The local copy now starts the audit service beside storage, and `pnpm local activity <client code> <record id>` prints one record's history, newest first: the time, who, what was done, and the names of the fields that changed, never their values. No screen shows the entries, and the shared pre-production environment does not run the service yet.

## What was decided, and what it beat

- **A change is never refused because its entry could not be written.** The back end gives the entry 600 ms, then saves the change anyway and writes one line in its own request log naming the entry it lost.
- **Entries keep 180 days**, the service's own constant, which this piece did not touch. A deleted client's entries outlast its deletion until their 180 days end.
