# For the product owner

This page is for you if you decide what credo does. After it you know where to see what works and what is missing, how to try a piece yourself, and how to ask us for a change.

**Start here:** read [what credo has today](what-credo-has.md). It says what works, what is not built, and what comes next, so you do not ask for something that already exists.

**Then read:** [can we take a real client yet?](ready-for-a-client.md). It gives today's answer and the gaps behind it, so you do not promise a client a start date we cannot keep.

**Try a piece yourself:** [check a piece without code](what-credo-has.md#check-a-piece-yourself-without-code). It walks you through one piece on [ridge](words.md#ridge), our shared test copy, so your yes or no rests on what you saw.

**What we give you:** a link and a password to a [demo copy](words.md#demo-copy), an invented company you can click freely. We send them by our own channel, never on a page.

**To ask for a change:** tell us, in your own words, what the person at the screen cannot do and why it matters. Your question and our answer are written on the [questions and answers](questions.md) page. If the answer changes the plan, you see it on the page of what credo has today.

**How you know it worked:** your question appears on the questions page with an answer.
