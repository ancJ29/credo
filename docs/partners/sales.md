# For the sales partner

This page is for you if you show credo to others. After it you know how to get your own link, what you can show, and what not to promise yet.

**First, ask us for your link and password.** They open your own [demo copy](words.md#demo-copy), an invented company, so you can click freely without touching anyone's real data. We send them by our own channel, never on a page.

**What you can show:** what works today, as [what credo has today](what-credo-has.md#what-works-today) lists it. Show one role at a time, since each person sees only the pages their permissions allow.

**What not to promise:** a start date, a price, or a feature on [the list of what is not built yet](what-credo-has.md#what-is-not-built-yet). Nothing runs for a real client yet, and [the readiness page](ready-for-a-client.md) says why. The price is on no page until we name it.

**Practise one piece before a meeting:** the [delivery photo](what-credo-has.md#example-the-delivery-photo). It takes five steps on a computer and a phone, so you know it works before a customer watches.

**How you know it worked:** you sign in to your copy, press "Delivered" on the phone, and your own photo shows on the office's page.
