# The credo docs map

This site explains credo to the people who work with it but do not all write its code. Open this
page first. Find the branch with your role on it, then read the pages under it from top to bottom.
Each page has one line saying who it is for and what it answers. If a page you need is not here,
ask the human, and your question will be answered in the questions page.

A page marked "not yet written" is promised and still empty. A page's name opens it.

## The map

- **Start here, for everyone**
  - [What credo is, without code](README.md): for anyone new; answers "what is this, and which page is mine?"
  - [Words we use](words.md): for anyone who meets a word they do not know; answers "what does this word mean?"
  - [Questions and answers](questions.md): for anyone with a question; answers "has someone asked this before?"
  - [What credo has today](what-credo-has.md): for partners who show or check credo; answers "what works, what is missing, and how do I check a piece myself?"
- **The product owner**
  - [For the product owner](product-owner.md): for the person who decides what credo does; answers "what do I read, and how do I ask for a change?"
  - [Can we take a client](../client-readiness.md): for the product owner; answers "is credo ready for a real client yet, and what is missing?"
- **The sales partner**
  - [For the sales partner](sales.md): for the person who shows credo to others; answers "what can I show, and where do I get my link?"
  - A demo, step by step: for the sales partner; answers "what do I open, say and point at in a first meeting?" **not yet written**
- **The operator**
  - [For the operator](operator.md): for the person who keeps credo running; answers "where do I start, and whom do I ask?"
  - [The operator guide](../operator-guide.md): for the operator; answers "how do I do each task, and what should I see after it?"
  - [The runbook](../../ops/README.md): for the operator who needs the reason behind a step; answers "why is it done this way?"
- **The new developer**
  - [For a new developer](developer.md): for someone joining the code; answers "what is the path to my first change?"
  - [First-time setup](../first-time-setup.md): for a new developer; answers "how do I get the tools and the code on my machine?"
  - [Quick start](../quick-start.md): for a new developer; answers "how do I see the app running with a demo company?"
  - [How credo is built](../architecture.md): for a new developer; answers "how does one click on screen become a saved record?"
  - [The code rules](../conventions/code.md): for a new developer; answers "what must I follow before I change any code?"
- **The QA engineer**
  - [For a tester](tester.md): for the person who checks that credo works; answers "what do I read, and where is each screen's question?"
  - [How we check our work](../conventions/qa.md): for a tester; answers "what is checked, when, and what counts as done?"
  - A first check, step by step: for a tester on the first day; answers "how do I run one check and write down what I found?" **not yet written**
