# For a tester

This page is for the person who checks that credo works. After it you know where to find what to check for one screen, where that screen's question is, and what your yes or no starts.

1. **Find the product.** Open [what credo has on hand](../products.md). Each product has a row. Its column "Open or start" says how to reach the product. Its last column names the plan that lists what the product must do.
2. **Read its cases.** Open that plan. Its case table lists, one line each, what the product must do. When two plans disagree about a screen, the newest plan wins.
3. **Answer the screen's question.** Each [walk](words.md#walk) is a recorded run through a screen, and the [walks page](words.md#walks-page) shows each screen of it full window with one question. You answer yes or no. A no asks for a short note on what was wrong. Ask us for the page's link. It needs no sign-in.
4. **Check a piece on [ridge](words.md#ridge)** when we hand you one. [What credo has today](what-credo-has.md) tells you how, with a card of at most six steps and one question.
5. **Know what counts as done.** [How we check our work](../conventions/qa.md) says what is checked, when, and what counts as done.

You know it worked when every screen of your product has a yes or a no from you, and every no has its note.
