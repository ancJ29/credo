# What credo is, without code

This section is for partners and anyone else who does not write code. After this page you know what credo is, which page is yours, and where to ask.

## What credo is

Credo is a system a company uses to take orders and deliver them, on a phone, a tablet or a computer, in Vietnamese or English. Office staff make a priced order and confirm it with a driver and a day. The driver sends "Delivered" with a photo. The office then compares the driver's day with the cash handed in, so a gap shows.

A second area is for the [operator](words.md#operator), the person who runs credo for clients. The operator adds a client and copies its sign-in link.
