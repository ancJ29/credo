# Milestone 3 of the app's layout: the nine palettes in Settings

## At a glance

The client's root picks the company's colour in Settings from the old app's nine palettes, shown as named swatches,
or types any colour as today. A palette picked is stored as its shade 7, the one colour the record already holds,
and every screen draws that palette's own ten shades, as the legacy's lookup does; any other colour is mixed as
today. Credo's colour is steel's shade 7 (`shared/src/config.ts#L33`), so the console takes steel's own shades. No
company's own ten shades until a client asks (the human, 11-03 17:47); no white header or menu (milestone 4).

The cheapest path is what `main` holds: one colour field (`ui/src/Settings.tsx#L172-L178`), one place that turns it
into ten shades (`ui/src/theme.ts#L84-L92`), the nine palettes and their lookup in the mock
(`ui/frame-mock/data.js#L3`, `ui/frame-mock/frame.js#L40-L47`). **Price:** $0 a month; about 47 machine minutes and
one full run.

## The slice

The UI alone: **the wire does not change.** The stored colour stays one `#rrggbb` (`shared/src/config.ts#L11-L12`,
`#L62`); the settings route, c-storage and the console's create (`bff/src/clients.ts#L34`) are untouched; no field
is added, so no default at the wire (`CLAUDE.md#L23`).

Decided here:

- **One home for the palettes, `ui/src/palettes.ts`:** the nine names and shades, from the mock's data; only the UI
  reads them.
- **The lookup sits in `shadesOf`:** a colour equal, in any case, to a palette's shade 7 returns that palette's ten
  shades; any other, the mix. `themeOf` calls it unchanged (`ui/src/theme.ts#L100`).
- **The picker:** above the colour field, one radio group, "Palette" ("Bảng màu"), of nine round swatches in the
  legacy's order, each named under it: terracotta, sandstone, forest, autumn, olive, emerald, steel, ocean, coin (Đất
  nung, Sa thạch, Rừng, Mùa thu, Ô liu, Ngọc lục bảo, Thép, Đại dương, Đồng xu). Picking one writes its shade 7 into
  the colour field; the swatch whose shade 7 the colour is shows checked, none for any other colour.
- **The walk picks terracotta** by its swatch's role and name, where it typed `#b4532a`
  (`walk/specs/piece-1.spec.ts#L31`, `#L109`, `#L144`); the mock's typed company takes `#d97757`
  (`ui/src/mock/fixtures.ts#L27-L31`). Any colour stays shown by every client walk: the demo's `#2f7d6d` is no stock
  shade (`shared/src/demo.ts#L30`). No sentence or question on the walks page changes.
