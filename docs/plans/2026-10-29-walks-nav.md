## What changes, as the human sees it

### One screen at a time

- The desk shows one screen: its number, its sentence in both languages, its drawing and its
  panel. Only its frames are drawn: one at PC, three with "All three".
- The address names the screen (`#<walk>-<screen>`, the list's link today), so a reload, Back or a
  pasted link opens it; with none, the page opens on the first screen waiting. A walk's card on
  the map opens its first screen waiting.
- The map, the build line, the partners' answers, the folded words and the list of answers whose
  screen left the walks stay as they are.
- *Rejected:* a long view kept as an option. It is today's page, whose price is the scroll and
  the frames. *Trigger to revisit:* the human asking to see screens side by side.

### Moving

- **Where you are**, one line atop the screen: the walk, "screen 12 of 16", the language, the
  width; under it Prev, Next and Next unanswered, which names where it goes and wraps at the end.
- **Keys:** J next, K previous, Y yes, N no (the reason box opens with the cursor in it), L the
  other language, U next unanswered. A key typed in a text box, or with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt, is left
  alone. One line under the bar names them.
- **The list:** language, width, the toggle, the bulk actions for all walks and the paste fixed at
  its top; each walk a heading with what is left and its own bulk actions, the walk in view open
  and the others folded; each screen on one line in number order, a "goes wrong" screen tagged in
  place, its two marks drawn as small squares, not buttons; the screen in view marked and kept in
  view. On a phone, "Screens" in the line atop opens the list over the page.
- **The bar:** one row on a PC; its segments shrink to fit (300 fit) and each opens its screen in
  its language (a click, not a Tab stop: the list is the keyboard's way); the note sits above it.
- **Tab** goes from the line atop to Yes and No, before any drawn screen. The drawn screens take
  no pointer, so a click or a wheel on one stays with the page and its keys.

### Both languages in one panel

The question, then a row a language: the language drawn with its large Yes and No; the other with
its mark and "Show in English" (or "Show in Tiếng Việt"), which draws the screen in it. The list's
switch, that button and L are one setting. *Rejected:* the list's VI and EN marks as the switch:
a mark that also switches reads as neither. *Rejected:* a panel for the drawn language alone,
today's, which walks every screen twice.

### One action at a time

- **The language drawn waits** (not answered, or asked again): Yes and No, in the page's teal;
  nothing else is teal.
- **A No:** the reason box opens with the cursor in it; Next unanswered stays plain until a reason
  is typed.
- **Answered:** Next unanswered, in teal, says where it goes: "Next: English" for this screen's
  other language, else "Next unanswered: screen 13".
- **Nothing waits on the page:** "All 72 answered", and in teal, Copy my answers on the human's
  page, Share on a partner's (its label box first when none is kept). The other stays in the bar.
- **Whose page:** one opened from disk (`file:`) is the human's, who opens `walks/` from disk;
  one served over the web, the Cloudflare address or a claude.ai page, is a partner's.
  *Rejected:* a flag in the partners' build, which would change the GitHub job this plan leaves
  alone; asking the viewer, one more question on a page meant to be shorter.
- **Answered, on a partner's page, means answered by that partner:** a choice on the page, or a
  row on record under the label kept in the browser. The human's answers show beside the screen
  there, as partners' answers show on the human's page, and mark nothing. *Why:* otherwise "Only
  unanswered", Next unanswered and "Yes to all" would skip every screen the human has answered,
  most of them, before the partner sees it.

### Answering many at once

- **On every page, partners' too** (call 1): beside Yes and No, "Yes for both languages (2)"; on
  each walk's heading in the list, "Yes to 12 · Clear 3"; atop the list, the same for all walks,
  "Yes to 41 · Clear 23". Each label carries its count, and is off at 0.
- **Unanswered** is waiting, or asked again after a yes. A bulk action never changes a choice made
  on the page, an answer on record, or a screen asked again after a No: a No is closed by looking
  ([QA](../conventions/qa.md#the-rules) rule 7). Clear takes back the choices made on the page
  alone, in the walk or in all walks.
- After it acts, the bar's note says "41 answered Yes · Undo". Undo puts the choices back as they
  were; the next change of any choice ends it.
- A bulk answer is an ordinary answer: the same line, key and fingerprint in the block and in
  Share's message as one given by hand.

### Paste last answers

- **"Paste last answers…"** atop the list opens a box. A person pastes an earlier "Copy my
  answers" block or Share message, and the page first says what it will do: "Fills 38 of 41; 3
  changed since; 2 not on this page; line 7 is not an answer line". "Fill" does it; Undo, as for
  a bulk action, takes it back.
- **A line fills when its key and its fingerprint are this page's**, whatever build its Build line
  names: the words it was given on are the words drawn now. A line whose words changed since is
  left unanswered, counted "changed since"; a key this page lacks, "not on this page". A No fills
  with its reason. A line the paste cannot read is named by its number and why, never guessed: the
  page reads with `pnpm answers`' own reader, unchanged.
- **The bulk rule holds:** a paste never changes a choice made on the page, an answer on record or
  a screen asked again after a No; those count as "already answered".
- **Whose answers:** the human's page fills from a block with no `By:` line alone; a partner's
  page from a Share message under its own label alone, and with none kept yet, the paste's label
  becomes the one kept. A block of anyone else is refused whole, saying whose, so a partner's
  answer never fills the human's page, nor the human's a partner's
  ([the sharing plan](2026-10-28-walks-share.md#how-partners-answer)).

### Only unanswered

- **A toggle atop the list,** off when the page opens, kept in the browser. While on, the list,
  Prev, Next, J and K hold only the screens with a language left to answer, each walk's heading
  says "done" when it has none; the bar keeps every segment, but those answered are no links.
- **The one screen in view is never an answered one,** but for the screen answered while in view,
  which stays until the person moves, so a No's reason can be typed and Undo pressed. Turning the
  toggle on over an answered screen moves to the next one left; an address naming an answered
  screen opens it and turns the toggle off, saying so.
- **With none left:** the screen's place says "Nothing left to answer", with Copy my answers on
  the human's page or Share on a partner's, and "Show all screens", which turns the toggle off.

- **jsdom lays nothing out.** The one-row bar, the one-line titles and the panel's place are CSS
  rules the tests read, not looks. *Guard:* the human's walk of the preview.

  human's or a partner's, and its line reads like one given by looking. What the reader of
  partners' answers should know: a partner's Yes says "nothing objected to", not "looked at"; a
  No is always someone's look, since no bulk action gives a No and a paste only carries a No
